Copyright and takedown requests

OpenPak does not host or distribute games, firmware or keys. If you believe something on openpak.org or an OpenPak service infringes your copyright or other rights, email contact@openpak.org with:

  • the work you believe is infringed, and the exact address (URL) or description of the material;
  • your name and contact details, and on whose behalf you act;
  • a statement that you believe in good faith the use is not authorised by the owner, its agent or the law;
  • a statement that the information in your notice is accurate and that you are the owner or authorised to act for the owner, and your physical or electronic signature.

We review every notice and remove material that infringes. If your material was removed and you believe that was a mistake, you can send a counter-notice to the same address.

For our source code on GitHub, you can also use GitHub’s DMCA process.
