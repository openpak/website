Last updated 23 October 2026.

These terms apply to anyone who runs an OpenPak region node (“operator”): one or more servers that relay game traffic for players near them, while accounts, friends and matchmaking stay with OpenPak. They add to the Terms of use. You accept them when you apply from your account page, and again each time a server you run joins the network.

Approval

  • A node serves nobody until OpenPak approves it. Approval is a decision by a person at OpenPak; meeting the figures below makes a node eligible, not approved.
  • Within the location policy below, OpenPak approves nodes for the locations it lists as wanted, and may keep you on a waitlist for anywhere else.
  • Each server gets its own key. Keep keys secret; if one leaks, tell us and we revoke that server.

Location policy

Applications are accepted from all locations except nodes inside mainland China and Russia. Licensing, national firewalls, sanctions and surveillance laws there conflict with these terms, so we cannot accept a node in either. Players there are served from neighbouring regions.

Hardware and network minimums

  • Each server must be dedicated to OpenPak: run nothing else on the box.
  • OpenPak Node OS (Fedora CoreOS) is the preferred install.

Hardware and address reports are self-declared by your servers and can be faked, so they are not proof: we use them only to decide whether a node is in rotation, and approval stays a decision by a person at OpenPak. Do not misreport them.

Player data

  • Do not log or keep player data beyond what running the relay needs at that moment. No packet captures, no IP address logs kept past their operational use, no per-player records.
  • Do not share, sell or analyse traffic or anything you learn from it.
  • Operational logs (errors, totals) are fine; keep them only as long as you need them to run the node, and never more than 7 days.

Data processing agreement

For the player data a node handles, you process it on OpenPak’s behalf, and you agree to the following:

  • You process player IP addresses and game traffic only to host rooms on your node.
  • You log nothing beyond operational need, and you delete those logs within 7 days.
  • You use the data for nothing else.
  • You notify OpenPak of any security incident affecting player data within 72 hours of learning of it, at {{contactEmail}}.
  • When you stop running a node, or your approval ends, you delete all player data you still hold.

No interference

Relay traffic exactly as it arrives. Do not modify, inject, block, delay or reorder it, do not run modified OpenPak software on a node, and do not use the shared secrets a node holds for anything else.

Revocation

OpenPak may remove a server or a node from rotation, revoke its keys or end its approval at any time, with or without notice, for any reason, including a breach of these terms. You may stop running a node at any time; please tell us first so players are not sent to it.

Security contact

Give a contact email that someone reads. Report a compromised server, a leaked key or any security problem at once to {{contactEmail}} or as described in our security policy. We may write to you about security issues and expect a reply.

No payment, no warranty

Running a node is voluntary and unpaid. OpenPak gives no warranty about the software or the network and is not liable for your hosting costs or for anything your servers do.
